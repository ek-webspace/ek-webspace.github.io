01 / PROFILE

Psychology as a foundation. Cognitive science as a bridge. Human–AI research as a direction.

My academic background began in psychology and developed into an interdisciplinary interest in cognition, behavior, and intelligent technology.

I am currently studying Cognitive Science at the University of Messina while building the methodological foundation required for quantitative and experimental research.

The central theme connecting my interests is simple: understanding what happens when human reasoning meets an increasingly capable artificial system.
